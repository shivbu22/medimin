Dolo 650 Tablet

Paracetamol (650mg)

Morekeyboard_arrow_down

MFD. By Micro Labs Ltd
Dolo 650 Tablet helps relieve pain and fever by blocking the release of certain chemical messengers that trigger these symptoms. It is used to treat headaches, migraines, toothaches, period pains, arthritis, and muscle aches, and to reduce fever associated with infections such as the common cold and flu. View More
